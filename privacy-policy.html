Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 13, 2026

Cambodian Adventist International School ("CAIS", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of students, parents, teachers, staff, and other authorized users of the CAIS Mobile Application and related services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

Information We Collect

Depending on your role and how you use the application, we may collect:

  • Full name and Username
  • Email address
  • Student, parent, teacher, and staff profile information
  • Attendance records and QR code attendance records
  • Academic grades and progress information
  • Class schedules, school announcements, and notifications
  • Profile photographs and documents uploaded by authorized users
  • Login, authentication information, and necessary device details for functionality and safety

We collect only the information necessary to provide educational and administrative services.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide secure access to the CAIS Mobile Application and authenticate users.
  • Display academic records, attendance, schedules, and announcements.
  • Record attendance through QR code scanning.
  • Allow users to upload profile photos and school-related documents.
  • Maintain school records and improve application performance, reliability, and security.
  • Respond to support requests.

Camera and Photo Access

The application may request permission to:

  • Access your photo library: So you can select profile photos or upload school-related images and documents.
  • Use the device camera: To scan QR codes for attendance and capture images for school-related purposes.

The application accesses these features only when you choose to use them.

Data Storage

Personal information is securely stored using our school's systems and trusted cloud service providers:

  • Structured application data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon.
  • Uploaded images and documents are stored securely using Google Cloud Storage.
  • Access to stored information is restricted to authorized users and school personnel with appropriate permissions.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may be shared only:

  • With authorized school administrators, teachers, students, and parents as required for educational services.
  • With trusted cloud service providers that host our infrastructure and storage.
  • When required by applicable law or legal process.

Data Security & Retention

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Although we take appropriate security measures, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide educational services, maintain school records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Children's Privacy

The CAIS Mobile Application is intended for use by students enrolled at Cambodian Adventist International School under the supervision of the school and, where applicable, their parents or guardians. Student information is collected solely for educational and administrative purposes.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws and school policies, users may request to access their personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion of information where appropriate, or ask questions regarding processing practices.

Third-Party Services

To operate our services, we use trusted third-party providers, including:

  • Neon: Secure PostgreSQL database hosting.
  • Google Cloud Storage: Storage for uploaded images and documents.
  • Expo technologies: Enabling features such as image selection and QR code scanning.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
