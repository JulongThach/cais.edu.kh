Message from the Principal

Dear Cambodia Adventist School Students, Parents, and CAIS Family,

Leader comes; leader goes, passing the responsibilities to the next one. The seed has been sown, the next person waters, but it is the Lord to make it grow. [1 Corinthians 3:6]

The primary philosophy of Seventh-day Adventist (SDA) education isn't just about getting good grades on standardized tests or securing a well-paid job. Of course, academic excellence is absolutely a priority; however, it is even more important to build on a deeply theological foundation for them; that is the restoration of the lost image into the character of God (image of God). Every child must be trained in the way they should go, and when he/she is old, he/she will not depart from it. [Proverbs 22:6]

It is a privilege and I am grateful that I was called to step into the role of principal for the next five years. Students, whether you are a returning student who knows these halls by heart, or a new student walking through the school campus for the very first time (perhaps with a backpack, full of books that looks a bit too big and heavy for you), I warmly welcome you. We are standing at the threshold of a beautiful new chapter. As we look ahead at the school years 2026-2027, we aren't just planning for the next school year; we are charting a new course to inspire minds, nurture souls, and to build a community that feels like family.

Over the coming years, our focus will be anchored in the core commitments to ensure all children don't just succeed as the Reflectors, but the Thinkers who possess the moral independence to evaluate facts, seek truth for themselves, stand for principle, and think critically.

In the Adventist school, being teachers are the highest call to create an environment where students would see God’s love daily, develop a strong moral compass, and learn the joy of selfless service to the country and the world. We acknowledge that all children are the heritage from God and they are equal in the eye of the Lord and will have the same opportunity to receive proper education that not only train the mind but also the heart. In a true educational framework, academic brilliance without a good character is considered a failure. The ultimate success of a student isn't measured by their GPA they get, the salary they will earn, or the prestige of their university acceptance, but it is measured by their integrity, their kindness, their work ethic, and their faithfulness to God, the Creator.

The world is changing rapidly, and our classrooms will reflect that. Our teachers will not be conformed to this world but will be transformed by renewing of their minds, that they may be proved what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God, [Romans 12:2]. From one school year to another school year, we will be improving our instructional curriculum with critical thinking, technology integration, integration of faith in learning, and creative problem-solving, ensuring our graduates are ready for whatever the future holds. We confidently ensure that CAIS is a place where students want to run into, not away from. We make it as a shelter of refuge for the students from whatever background. We are committed to fostering a campus culture of kindness, mental well-being, and mutual respect where every single student feels seen, accepted, and valued.

To our inspiring students, whether you are eager to get back to the classroom or secretly wishing that summer vacation lasted just a little bit longer, I am so glad that you are here. My challenge to you for this 2026–2027 chapter is simple: Don't be afraid to grow; make mistakes, ask big questions, be kind to the person sitting next to you, and discover the unique gifts God has given you. The future is incredibly bright at Cambodia Adventist School. Let’s step into this new era together with faith in our hearts and a smile on our faces, go forth and conquer.

To the parents, I would like to thank you for entrusting us with your most beloved, greatest treasures. Education is a team sport, and we cannot do this without your support. Your involvement in their education has a great impact on your child’s success. We promise that school’s door will always be open to you. Let’s work hand-in-hand over these years to celebrate our children's triumphs and support them physically, socially, emotionally, and spiritually through their challenges.

May God richly bless our school as we begin this remarkable journey.

Sincerely in His Service with prayers and anticipation,

Mr. Sieng Kimsreng

Principal of Cambodia Adventist School
